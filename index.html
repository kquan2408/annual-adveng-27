I) Course Descriptor

English Advanced - 12th Grade

English Advanced is a course designed to strengthen and develop students’ English language proficiency through the integrated practice of the four main language skills: reading, listening, writing, and speaking, as well as vocabulary development.

The course uses Oxford Discover Futures as a supplementary learning resource to help students develop their language competencies through a variety of activities and exercises that encourage meaningful learning, application, and practice.

Section A (A1–B1)

Students develop foundational English skills and strengthen their ability to understand and communicate in familiar and everyday situations. They expand their vocabulary and improve their reading, listening, writing, and speaking skills through guided practice.

Section B (B2–Low C1)

Students strengthen their intermediate to advanced English skills by developing greater fluency, expanding their vocabulary, understanding more complex texts, expressing ideas clearly, and producing more organized written and spoken communication.

Section C (High C1–C2)

Students refine their advanced English proficiency by engaging in complex reading and listening tasks, producing sophisticated written texts, expressing nuanced ideas fluently and accurately, and using a wide range of vocabulary.

Key Pedagogical Principles

  • Inquiry-based learning
  • Student-centered learning
  • Exploration of global values and perspectives
  • A focus on critical thinking rather than memorization
  • Building strong student-teacher relationships and reflection
  • Cooperation, collaboration, and synergy

III) Academic Competencies, Achievement Indicators & CM Competencies

Subject competencies combined with Colegio Montano institutional standards

Academic Competencies & Achievement Indicators

1. Conversational & Informational Exchange

Generates conversations to exchange specific information about learning and environment topics orally and written.

  • Proposes oral and written conversation to exchange essential and personal information about everyday topics.
  • Uses culturally acceptable vocabulary, idiomatic expressions, and gestures.
  • Expresses wants, doubts, uncertainty, and needs using learned material orally and written.
  • Answers questions posed both orally and written.

2. Text Production & Grammar Accuracy

Produces oral or written texts independently to communicate needs and learning purposes with appropriate grammar and vocabulary.

  • Speaks using accurate vocabulary, pronunciation, and grammar.
  • Requests help or information for researching purposes.
  • Makes inferences from simple or complex oral and/or written passages.
  • Makes daily journal entries in short writings and composes texts with accurate grammar, spelling, and vocabulary.

3. Cultural Engagement & Interculturality

Improves ability to engage with other cultures and develops effectively in global environments.

  • Interacts with other cultures respecting differences and diversity.
  • Uses cultural contributions of other people to enrich their own culture.
  • Compares festivity activities in English-speaking countries with Guatemalan holiday traditions.

IV) Achievement Indicators & Evaluation Criteria

Criteria of evaluation and performance benchmarks

Oral & Conversational Assessment

Evaluates oral fluency, accuracy in pronunciation, structured arguments during public speaking sessions, and interactive exchanges during group discussions and peer work.

Written & Analytical Assessment

Measures correctness in complex grammar structures (cleft sentences, participle clauses, noun clauses), persuasive essay writing, and proper academic referencing.

Research & Listening Evaluation

Assesses the student's ability to apply the SQ3R method for research, evaluate scientific credibility, extract specific information from lectures, and synthesize specialist vocabulary.

Soft Skills & Project Work

Appraises leadership performance, teamwork coordination, emotional intelligence in conflict resolution, and the execution of collaborative unit projects such as launching a business product.
